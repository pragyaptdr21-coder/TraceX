4. DETECTION ALGORITHMS
- `velocity_detector.py`: Windowed self-join. Output: Account, timestamps, amounts. False Positives: Extremely low due to rigid strict windowing and >90% boundaries. Matches PDF exactly.
- `collector_detector.py`: Distinct fan-in aggregations. Threshold: >=5 (Engineering choice). False Positives: Moderate if used standalone; strong when combined with cycles.
- `distributor_detector.py`: Distinct fan-out aggregations. Threshold: 3-7 bounds (PDF). Matches PDF exactly. 
- `terminal_detector.py`: Regex/Substring matching on string metadata. Output: IPs, Devices, Narrations. Matches PDF exactly.
- `cycle_detector.py`: Structural 3-hop join (A->B->C->A). Threshold: Hop limit is an Engineering choice to prevent full graph explosions.
- `trail_engine.py`: BFS traversal queue over DuckDB querying. Matches PDF exactly.
