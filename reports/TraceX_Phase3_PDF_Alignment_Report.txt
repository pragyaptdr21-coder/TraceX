2. ALGORITHM & FALSE-POSITIVE CONTROL
The architecture leverages DuckDB's vectorized analytical power to group, join, and filter millions of rows natively. False positives are heavily mitigated by relying on *strict behavioral intersections* (like temporal disbursement bounds and distinct counterparty counts) instead of simple transaction volume. A Mule Risk Index exposes exactly why an account was scored, providing a transparent chain of evidence for every flagged entity.
