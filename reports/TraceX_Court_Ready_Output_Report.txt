Scope
-----
Added an explicit evidence-backed Section 91 Notice workflow without removing Case Diary, Freeze Requisition, existing PDF exports, graph, timeline, evidence, or detection features.

Validation basis
----------------
- Real local DuckDB transaction data was used by automated tests.
