5. KNOWN LIMITATIONS & CONCESSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Distributor L2: Expanding the strict 5-minute heuristic in L2 causes massive fan-out. We mitigated this by bounding layer sizes to 2000 nodes during BFS traversal to maintain UI responsiveness and server stability.
- AI Hallucination Guard: To ensure deterministic outputs and prevent fabricated evidence, AI context is strictly limited to 50 transactions. Larger trails require manual subgraph isolation before generating the Case Diary.
- Unicode in PDF: The `fpdf` library relies on latin-1 encoding, which may require transliteration for native scripts.
- Data Storage: Uses local DuckDB storage which is highly performant but requires a managed DB (e.g., PostgreSQL/BigQuery) for horizontal scaling in a production multi-tenant environment.
